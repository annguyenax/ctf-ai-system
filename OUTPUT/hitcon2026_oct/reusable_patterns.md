# Reusable patterns

- When an integer algebra product is published only projectively modulo a large power of two, build the modular lattice from the projective coordinates and recover the shortest integer representative.
- If the hidden coordinates lie in a reduced lattice basis with small coefficients, use a multiplicative norm constraint to get a congruence in the 36 quadratic monomials for eight coefficients. LLL can reveal the rank-one outer product.
- In octonions, preserve multiplication parentheses. Moufang and alternative identities can peel factors from a deliberately structured block product, while reducing coordinates modulo each generator norm keeps the search small.
- Verify by forward replaying the exact non-associative operation order and by re-encrypting the recovered plaintext; a flag-shaped AES plaintext alone is not enough.
- Keep online scoreboard status separate from cryptographic verification when the platform is unavailable.
